How the score works and where the data came from

Must-haves (gates): G1 3 BHK · G2 new, or first owner within about a year of OC · G3 possession by May 2028 · G4 two independent parkings (negotiable) · G5 lift · G6 PMC water or a society that manages water well (negotiable) · G7 within 30 min of the temple at peak · G8 budget about ₹1.5–2 Cr, hard limit ₹2.6 Cr · G9 not a lone building beside a slum or nala.

Score: twelve criteria rated 0–5 and weighted (walkability 14, traffic 10, cleanliness 10, society 10, water 8, shops on foot 8, temple and community 8, value 8, healthcare 7, flat specifics 7, possession and legal 7, Kothrud 3), scaled to 100, minus up to 10 for serious red flags.

Drive times are estimates: free-flow routing times 1.9 for evening traffic. Distances to parks, nalas, slums and roads come from OpenStreetMap and are capped at the search radius, shown as "800+ m".

Sources: NoBroker, SquareYards, MagicBricks, Dwello, Housiey, Houssed, HomeBazaar, builder websites, MahaRERA mirror sites, news and forums. MahaRERA itself and Housing.com could not be reached from the research machine, so OC status is often unconfirmed.
